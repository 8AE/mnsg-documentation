## How it works

This pre-fight state belongs to the health-bearing entity-0x1B0 child in Koryuta flight room 0x155.

When the absolute difference between the player-object and child-object Z coordinates is less than 200.0, the state writes transient save flag 0x019F, sets D_8015CDC0, and schedules func_08002F58_704058 as the first combat state. This is a one-axis Z threshold, not a full 3D distance.

A separate encounter camera task reads D_8015CDC0 and, when set, starts its camera actor and allocates a camera handle. That reader does not repeat the local proximity check.
