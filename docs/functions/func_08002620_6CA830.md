## How it works

The allocator establishes projectile +0xDC = visible root before this scheduled initializer runs.

Initialization installs entity 0xCC, one of the two travelling callbacks func_0800284C_6CAA5C or func_08002A40_6CAC50, and the common actor post. Trail, impact and phase-effect children are separate task families.
