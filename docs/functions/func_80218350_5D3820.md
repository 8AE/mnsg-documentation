## How it works

Status bit 0x00040000 at actor+0x68 enables a fast one-point health subtraction at byte +0x8D without the usual attacker pointer. The normal path checks actor flags, hit bit 0x80, attacker data and damage type.

Nonzero health sets status bit 0 and may install/invoke the actor-specific +0x90 callback. Zero health enters generic death. The inner routine does not clear the synthetic pending-damage bit the same way the outer func_80218E7C wrapper does.

Some bosses use generic health only as a damage carrier. Dharmanyo separately consumes lives at +0xD1, while Tsurami uses its custom remaining-health callback.
