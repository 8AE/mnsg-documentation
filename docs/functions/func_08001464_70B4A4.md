## How it works

Controller data is reached through actor+0xD0. Its phase is data+0x08 and owned child is data+0x2C. Flag 0x069 selects post-fight topology; dialogue completion flag 0x033 alone does not fully describe the encounter layout.

Phase 2 is the idle entrance-trigger topology. In an already completed encounter with no active fight flag 0x07A, a specifically owned idle child can be rebuilt by clearing that child and returning the controller to phase zero.
