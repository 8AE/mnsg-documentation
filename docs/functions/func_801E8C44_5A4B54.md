## How it works

The global next-task pointer is at task +0x00 and the unsigned 16-bit hierarchy depth is at +0x20. A subtree continues while each next task has depth greater than its manager; stop at a peer or ancestor.

The scan counts descendants whose byte at +0x64 equals the selector. It does not test byte +0x65.

A new child becomes countable when its kind is initialized, before its first scheduled update.
