## How it works

This callback belongs to the health-bearing root combat AI sequence and is installed in the task AI slot at +0x0C.

Its individual attack, timer, motion formula and side effects have not been established. The hexadecimal state label identifies this entry without assigning an unsupported attack name.

The combat sequence is separate from introduction states 0x1A50..0x1D0C, hit reactions 0x2E00..0x2FA4 and defeat states 0x2FFC..0x3A00.
