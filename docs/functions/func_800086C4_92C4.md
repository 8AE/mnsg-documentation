## How it works

The helper stores its second argument (MIPS a1) as the display object's descriptor pointer at +0x34.

It stores its third argument (MIPS a2) as the display object's descriptor-bank pointer at +0x14.

The descriptor and bank pointers occupy separate fields and are both used by native display paths.
