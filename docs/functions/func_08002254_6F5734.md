## How it works

No sound call was found in this travel-door completion callback.

This observation is limited to the inspected file_43 travel-door family and does not establish that all door-close or door-completion paths are silent.

After waiting for animation bit 2 at object +0x7C, the File43 completion path clears interaction state and writes the low byte of task halfword +0xD8 to full-system +0xCF898. This is a selector latch, not a destination room ID.

A separate common transition controller, actor ID 0x08C, matches the selector and queues its own packed destination fields. Do not derive a destination room from the visual door payload.
