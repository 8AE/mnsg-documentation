## How it works

Installs entity 0xCB, recurring AI func_08004ED0_6B8170 and the common actor post.

The low five bits of attack flags at task +0xE8 identify modes 0x01, 0x02, 0x04, 0x08 and 0x10. Their full mapping to individual attack trajectories is not established.

Calls func_8021A310_5D57E0 during initialization, setting object rotation halfwords +0x14, +0x16 and +0x18 to the native special value 0x8000. This value is not an ordinary 0..1023 angle.

The mode-0x01 attack installs reflection callback func_08005B1C_6B8DBC in task +0x90. That callback schedules func_08005B50_6B8DF0; the latter creates a replacement travelling attack in mode 0x10, copies the original boss-root link at task +0xD4 into the replacement and removes the original projectile. The allocator owner for the replacement is the original projectile, distinct from its copied boss-root link.
