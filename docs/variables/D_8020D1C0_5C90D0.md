`D_8020D1C0_5C90D0` · **Variable**

Three floating-point camera components used for gameplay projection.

| Runtime address | ROM address | Section |

| `0x8020D1C0` | — | `ABSOLUTE_SYMS` |

## Signature

```c
extern Vec3f D_8020D1C0_5C90D0;
```

## How it works

Exposed as a vector of three consecutive floats and paired with D_8020D1D0_5C90E0 by the gameplay camera projection convention.

The observed use identifies a camera offset/vector; it does not establish a complete camera matrix contract or prove the same interpretation in every cutscene.

## Known values

### Camera-vector components {#vector-components}

This is direct Vec3f storage, not a pointer variable. Values are continuous single-precision coordinates, not room or camera-mode IDs.

| Offset | Storage type | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `+0x00` | `float` | X component. |
| `+0x04` | `float` | Y component. |
